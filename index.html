Review Bot, built for enterprise reality

The bot reviews pull requests primarily as a diff. It fetches only the minimal, targeted context required to understand and validate a change. Less noise, fewer hallucinations, more actionable feedback.

Diff-first focus
Reviews changed lines and their immediate impact, not the entire repo.
Targeted context retrieval
Pulls definitions, interfaces, helpers, configs, or examples only when needed.
Rules + ADRs + internal policies
Applies organization-specific best practices with “why here, why now”.
Structured output
Must fix / Should fix / Nit with line-level references and impact tags.
Patch-level suggestions
Provides diffs/snippets, not vague advice you have to interpret.
Confidence & verification
Explicit certainty per finding and what must be checked if context is incomplete.

High-signal review, not generic checklists

Best practices are applied in two layers: universal engineering standards (security, error handling, validation, backward compatibility, performance, readability) plus your internal rules (coding standards, ADRs, compliance docs). The bot explains why each point matters here, in this diff, for this change.
